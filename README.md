# One More Step (Kolejny mały krok)

**3D Space Educational Game**

Made in Unity 3D for the [Polish Space Agency, POLSA "Play Space" competition](https://plinspace.pl/edukacja/konkursy/konkurs-zagraj-w-kosmos?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

## What is this game about

**One More step** is a short first-person **educational space simulation game** made in Unity 3D.

In this game we play as a novice astronaut aboard the International Space Station (ISS) and experience our first spacewalk through a mission to fix an air leak.


<br><br>
## 🛠 Programming Features
###  Player & Movement
-  Zero-gravity first-person controller  
-  Spacewalk mechanic with rails + tether system 
   -  Carabiner smoothly slides along rails 
   -  Player must repin tether when one rail ends  

###  **Interactions**
- Interactable system to: 
   - Talk to NPCs
   - Pick up / drop objects
   - Put on spacesuit
   - Enter / exit ISS
   - Pin/Unpin Carabiner
   - Move between rails
   - Repair air leak


###  Spacewalk Mechanic
- Astronaut is tethered (rope with carabiner)
- Carabiner attaches to rails mounted on the ISS exterior (rails form a path to an air leak)
- Carabiner slides along rails while the player moves to add realism (looks like the rope pulls on a carabiner)
- Player must unpin & repin the tether from ne rail to another to continue progressing
- Unique mechanic: player must stay attached to the ISS to remain safe during a spacewalk 

###  Mini-Games
**Repair Mini-Game** inspired by Stardew Valley’s fishing system
